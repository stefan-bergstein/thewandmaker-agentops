Six layers.

  1. 1Start with a hardened platformOpenShift · restricted SCCs, everything as codein the shop
  2. 2Isolate the runtimeone pod per digital apprentice · no kernel isolation (Kata) yetnot in this demo
  3. 3Sandbox the processOpenShell · a policy per binary, no keys insidechapter 1
  4. 4Guard the conversationNeMo Guardrails · MCP gatewaychapters 1 and 3
  5. 5Attack yourself firstour red-team campaign · a gate before every releasechapter 3

No clicks. Red Hat's whitepaper "Defense in depth for agentic AI" names six layers; this is the shop measured against them, honestly. Say it from the bottom up, one breath per layer. 1 the platform: OpenShift, every shop pod under the restricted security context, the sandbox under a narrow one instead of privileged, no service account token in it, and all of it as code. 2 is the one we did not build: each digital apprentice has a pod of his own, but no VM around it; OpenShift sandboxed containers (Kata) would be that layer. 3 is chapter 1: OpenShell, a policy per binary, credential-free inference. 4 is the rails of chapter 3 plus the MCP gateway in front of the tools. 5 is the second oracle; in the product that is Garak, ours is a campaign runner with the shop's own rules. 6 is half done: you saw the traces and the audit records in chapter 2, but the tools still share one key held by the gateway; short-lived workload identity (SPIFFE) is not in the shop.
